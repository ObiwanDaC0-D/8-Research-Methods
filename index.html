SAMPLING LAB

Sampling variability and selection bias

Generate a synthetic population with a known mean. Compare a simple random sample against a systematically selected sample that overrepresents high values.

The selection-biased sample is deliberately unrealistic to make the distinction between random error and systematic bias visible. Increasing sample size alone does not eliminate selection bias.
